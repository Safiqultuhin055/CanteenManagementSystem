Dashboard

The home screen shows live counts: employees, menu items, orders today, sales revenue, kitchen queue, and advance balance. Use quick-action tiles for POS, Kitchen, Distribution, and Reports.

Point of Sale (POS)

The POS is the sales counter screen. It supports two ways to build an order — a manual touch/scan flow, and a hands-free Bangla voice assistant. Both end the same way: one order, one token, one auto-printed slip.

Manual employee sale

1 Scan (or type) the employee RFID card number. The card resolves by exact, case-insensitive, or partial UID match.
2 Confirm the employee name, department, and available advance + credit balance shown on screen.
3 Add menu items by category and adjust quantities. Sold-out / expired items are blocked; remaining stock is shown per item.
4 Press Complete Sale. One token number is generated and the customer slip prints automatically.

Guest / cash

Turn on guest mode for a walk-in cash sale — no card needed. The order is recorded as a CASH sale with customer name Guest Customer. Employee sales are CARD and deduct from advance balance first, then credit.

One token per sale: however many items you add, checkout creates a single daily token (Token #N, reset each business day) that the kitchen and distribution monitor both track.

Voice ordering assistant (Bangla)

When any AI provider is configured (Admin → API integrations — Claude, Gemini, or a self-hosted LLM), a Voice button appears on the POS. The customer speaks in Bangla, a female sales-assistant persona replies by voice, and the order is built, confirmed, and printed hands-free.

Step-by-step: how the order becomes an invoice

Under the hood, each turn of the conversation is one round-trip to the AI; the final confirm turns the spoken order into a real, paid invoice.

#What the customer seesWhat the system does
1Assistant greets and asks for the cardVoice modal opens, speaks the welcome, and starts the bn-BD microphone; without a card it asks the customer to punch one.
2Customer punches the RFID cardThe card is resolved to an employee; name, department, and balance are shown before ordering.
3Customer speaks an item + quantitySpeech becomes text and is sent to the active AI provider (Claude, Gemini, or self-hosted).
4Assistant repeats the order backThe model returns the full order via the submit_turn tool; the system caps quantities to live stock and computes the total.
5Customer says "কনফার্ম"ready_to_confirm becomes true; items are pushed into the cart and checkout runs.
6Token announced by voiceA daily token, order, order-details, payment, and kitchen-queue rows are created in one atomic transaction; balance is deducted.
7Slip prints; token turns ReadyThe 80 mm slip (token + barcode) prints; the token joins the live canteen monitor and blinks under Ready when the kitchen marks it done.
Requirements: the voice button only appears when an AI provider is active. Speech recognition needs a Chromium-based browser and microphone permission; Bangla text-to-speech is served by the app, so no Windows Bangla voice is required. If speech is unavailable, the customer can type instead.

Kitchen (KDS)

  • New orders appear with token number and line items.
  • Mark orders Preparing then Ready.
  • Ready orders appear in the distribution queue.

Distribution

  • Counter display lists tokens waiting for pickup.
  • Verify token and employee before handover.
  • Mark Completed to close the order.

Balance and cards

Each employee has advance balance and optional credit. Allocations are managed under Admin → Balance. Cards must be ACTIVE for POS.
