Overview

CanteenSys manages employee canteen sales from card scan through kitchen preparation to token distribution. Each role sees only the menus assigned by your administrator.

Sign in

1 Open /users/login/ in your browser.
2 Enter your username and password.
3 After sign out you return to the login page; sign in again to continue.
Seed admin: superadmin — change the default password before production use.

Dashboard

The home screen shows live counts: employees, menu items, orders today, sales revenue, kitchen queue, and advance balance. Use quick-action tiles for POS, Kitchen, Distribution, and Reports.

Point of Sale (POS)

Employee sale

  • Scan or enter the employee RFID card number.
  • Confirm employee name and available balance.
  • Add menu items by category; adjust quantities.
  • Complete checkout — a token number is generated for kitchen and distribution.

Guest / cash

Use guest card or cash payment as configured. Guest cards track deposit and loaded balance separately.

Kitchen (KDS)

  • New orders appear with token number and line items.
  • Mark orders Preparing then Ready.
  • Ready orders appear in the distribution queue.

Distribution

  • Counter display lists tokens waiting for pickup.
  • Verify token and employee before handover.
  • Mark Completed to close the order.

Balance and cards

Each employee has advance balance and optional credit. Allocations are managed under Admin → Balance. Cards must be ACTIVE for POS.
